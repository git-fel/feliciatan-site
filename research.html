Human-AI interaction: resource constraints, trust and judgment

AI Usage Limits and Token Anxiety: The Effects of Resource Visibility on Model Selection, Prompting, and Trust in AI Recommendations Preprint Tan, F., Avula, S., & Nov, O. A survey of frequent AI users (N = 953) and an experimental evaluation (N = 135) found that resource visibility significantly increased token anxiety, model-switching, and exploratory behavior without improving resource utilization or prompting strategy. Resource visibility does not directly translate to resource manageability.

The Evaluation–Contestation Gap: Users Rarely Contest Misfitting Conversational AI Advice and Adapt It Themselves Preprint Tan, F., Noh, H., Messerschmidt, M. A., Das Swain, V., & Nov, O. In a survey of 737 users who recalled a recent AI advice-seeking episode, 76.2% of those who rated the advice an imperfect fit did not contest it, including 70.0% of those who evaluated it critically. We term this the evaluation–contestation gap.

The Impact of Response Latency and Task Type on Human-LLM Interaction and Perception ACM CHI 2026 Tan, F., Messerschmidt, M. A., Yin, W., & Nov, O. A controlled experiment varying time-to-first-token latency (2, 9, 20 seconds) across Creation and Advice tasks. User interaction behaviors were robust to latency, yet participants who experienced 2-second latencies rated the LLM’s outputs less thoughtful and useful than those who experienced 9- or 20-second latencies.
LLMsLatency
Counting the Wait: Effects of Temporal Feedback on Downstream Task Performance and Perceived Wait-Time Experience during System-Imposed Delays ACM CHI 2026 Tan, F., & Nov, O. An online experiment (n = 425) on temporal feedback during system-imposed waits. Remaining-Time feedback increased frustration relative to Elapsed-Time, and No Time Display made waits feel longer, without differences in post-wait task performance.

An email-writing system with structured communication factor exploration, granular editing controls, and adaptive reuse of successful strategies. Signals of Aggression: Modelling Multimodal Cues and Perceptual Effects in Virtual Agents ACM CHI 2026 Ong, S., Koh, A., Cai, S., Tan, F., Chia, P. & Khoo, E. T. A psychologically grounded model that parametrizes language, voice, body movement and facial expressions across four aggression levels for intelligent virtual agents, evaluated in two studies with 38 flight attendants. Coordinated multimodal integration produced perceptually robust aggression levels, with body and facial cues carrying most weight.

Attention, curiosity and learning

Curious Shorts: Curiosity-Driven Exploration and Learning on Short-Form Video Platforms ACM CHI 2025 Tan, F., Ram, A., Messerschmidt, M. A., Dissanayake, H. A., & Nanayakkara, S. A conceptual framework that extends the Hook Model to enhance curiosity-driven exploration and incidental learning on short-form video platforms. Specific, question-driven prompts significantly boosted curiosity and encouraged more focused and intentional viewing. In a realistic viewing environment interspersed with entertainment videos, engagement remained high while learning benefits diminished.
Short-form videoLearning
AudioXtend: Assisted Reality Visual Accompaniments for Audiobook Storytelling During Everyday Routine Tasks ACM CHI 2024 Tan, F., Xu, P., Ram, A., Suen, W. Z., Zhao, S., Huang, Y., & Hurter, C. An approach to augment audiobook experiences during daily tasks by integrating glanceable, AI-generated visuals through optical see-through head-mounted displays. The visual augmentations preserved users’ primary task efficiency while enhancing immediate auditory content recall by 33.3% and 7-day recall by 32.7%.

Health, wellbeing and care

ViFeed: Repurposing Screen Time to Foster Mealtime Mindfulness and Healthy Eating ACM CHI 2025 Honorable Mention Chen, Y., Tan, F., Wang, Z., Liu, X., Zhang, J., Huang, Y., Zhao, S., & Yen, C. Strategic video design to promote slow eating and food awareness during screen-based dining. Subtle video speed manipulations promoted slower eating (by 15.31%) and controlled food intake (by 9.65%) while maintaining meal satiation and satisfaction. A week-long field deployment demonstrated efficacy in fostering food awareness, food appreciation, and sustained engagement.

Facilitating Virtual Reality Integration in Medical Education: A Case Study of Acceptability and Learning Impact in Childbirth Delivery Training ACM CHI 2024 Liu, C., Tan, F., Zhao, S., Kanneganti, A., Gosavi, A. T., & Khoo, E. T. A study with 117 medical students on the acceptability and educational effectiveness of VR for vaginal childbirth delivery training, contrasted with manikin-based methods. VR showed a significant 24.9% improvement in knowledge scores, but significantly lower self-reported feasibility scores in confidence, usability, enjoyment, feedback and presence, indicating low acceptance.

Heads-up computing and human augmentation

Assistive Augmentation: Fundamentally Transforming Human Ability ACM Interactions, Jan 2025 Tan, F., Gupta, C., Rajendran, D. P., Maes, P., & Nanayakkara, S. Assistive augmentation is a subtle yet significant shift, moving from seeing technology as a separate tool to considering it an integrated extension of the human body, mind, and identity. Two dimensions, ability (physical, cognitive, perceptual) and integration (bodily, temporal, identity, sociocultural), provide a structured framework to fundamentally expand human potential.

Not All Spacings are Created Equal: The Effect of Text Spacings in On-the-go Reading Using Optical See-Through Head-Mounted Displays ACM CHI 2023 Zhou, C., Fennedy, K., Tan, F., Zhao, S., & Shao, Y. Increasing spacing within the text significantly enhances mobile reading on OHMDs in both simple and complex navigation scenarios, with benefits mainly from increasing inter-line spacing, not inter-word spacing. Compared with existing positioning strategies, increased inter-line spacing improves reading speed (11.9% faster), walking speed (3.7% faster), and switching between reading and navigation (106.8% more accurate and 33% faster).
